Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. What DOXFRAME does

DOXFRAME provides image and PDF utilities, user accounts, usage tracking and optional Pro subscriptions. This page explains the current data flow of the production build.

2. Files and browser processing

The current browser-first tools process source files locally in your browser whenever the tool can do so. The usage API receives only the tool name when a job is reserved; it does not receive the source file contents. The production build does not create a permanent upload area for browser-first tools.

Some advanced conversions may depend on browser libraries and can behave differently for complex documents. If a future tool requires server-side file processing, DOXFRAME should disclose that data flow before the file is transmitted.

3. Account information

If you create an account, DOXFRAME stores your name, email address, a password hash and salt, session records, plan status and subscription identifiers. Passwords are never stored as plaintext.

Job history stores the selected tool, status and timestamp, not the contents of your files.

4. Usage limits and security

DOXFRAME records daily job counters to enforce the Free (10 jobs/day) and Pro (500 jobs/day) limits. Anonymous usage is associated with a privacy-preserving server-side hash of the request IP and a secret pepper; the raw IP is not stored in the usage table.

5. Billing

Pro subscriptions are designed to use Razorpay. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider. DOXFRAME stores subscription identifiers and status needed to grant or revoke Pro access. Payment secrets are kept as Cloudflare Worker secrets and are not placed in browser code.

6. Feedback and service providers

Feedback submissions may include the category, selected tool, optional email address and message. Cloudflare Turnstile can be enabled to reduce automated spam. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information through feedback.

DOXFRAME uses service providers needed to operate the service. Cloudflare provides hosting/security and may process security and anti-abuse signals; Razorpay handles payment processing when Pro billing is enabled; and Resend is used to deliver account-verification and password-reset emails. Each provider may process the information necessary for its service under its applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Depending on provider infrastructure, account configuration and the user's location, personal data may be processed in countries other than India. DOXFRAME will use applicable contractual, technical or other safeguards required by the laws that apply to the processing.

7. Cookies and sessions

Signed-in sessions use a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie. The cookie is used to keep you signed in and is not intended to contain your password.

8. Retention and deletion

Session records expire after 30 days. Job history is automatically removed after 90 days, feedback after 180 days, webhook events after 90 days, rate-limit records after 2 days and anonymous daily-usage counters after 7 days. You can request account deletion at support@doxframe.com; authenticated users can also delete their account from the account area after password confirmation. Deletion removes the account, sessions, job history, feedback linked to the account and subscription records held by DOXFRAME. An active Pro subscription must be cancelled before account deletion.

9. Your rights and grievance redressal

You can stop using the service at any time. Where applicable under the law governing your personal data, you may request information/access, correction, completion or updating, erasure/deletion, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the processing basis. Authenticated requests may require reasonable verification before action is taken.

For a privacy request or grievance, use the Feedback section on the DOXFRAME home page and clearly state that the message is a privacy/data-protection request. DOXFRAME may request additional information needed to verify the requester and will handle the request under the timelines and exceptions required by applicable law. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive secrets.

10. Legal basis, retention and lawful processing

Personal data is processed only for lawful purposes connected with providing, securing, improving and administering the service, or where another lawful basis applies. Data is retained only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, security, contractual obligations, accounting or other legal requirements, subject to the retention periods described above and any lawful exceptions.

11. Important note

This page describes the current technical implementation and is not legal advice. Review it with appropriate professional advice before public commercial launch, especially if you serve users in jurisdictions with specific privacy, consumer or data-transfer requirements.
