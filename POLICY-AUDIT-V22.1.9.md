## Remaining deployment configuration
The build intentionally does not invent a new public domain, legal entity name, registered address, or jurisdiction. Existing `doxframe.com` canonical/contact references remain until the operator supplies the actual production domain/legal details. Those values should be replaced before launch if the production domain or legal operator differs.
